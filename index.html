How the app applies the constitution

4

R300 minimum, due by the 7th. A month counts as paid if any money arrives in that month. If the first payment of the month is dated after the 7th, the month is marked late. Paying more than R300 adds to your savings.

Paying less than R300 is not a skipped month, but the shortfall shows as arrears.

5

R50 penalty for a skipped month. A month that ends with no payment at all is missed and costs R50. The next payment settles the penalty first, then the savings. Catching up after one missed month is R300 + R50 + R300 = R650.

Penalties are fees to the Stokvel, so they are not part of anyone's savings.

6

Payout on 20 December. Each member receives their savings plus their share of interest.

7

Early withdrawal. A withdrawing member gets their savings on the day they leave, less any penalties still owed (7.4). No contributions are due after the withdrawal month. They only share in interest if they contributed for more than 7 months (7.3).

Stopping without withdrawing (7.2). Each missed month adds a R50 penalty. Penalties still unpaid on 20 December are deducted from that member's savings.

8

Interest. Interest is shared in proportion to savings, only among members who contributed in more than 7 months of the year (8 or more).

9

Expulsion. Three missed months in a row means automatic expulsion. The member forfeits all savings, which stay with the Stokvel. The secretary records it in writing (9.3).

10

Office bearers. Chairperson Zwivhuya Rammela, Treasurer Vhanga Mudzunga, Secretary Ovhonala Makhera. The treasurer records payments and the interest figure here.
